# Host guide: Last Person Standing

**Site:** https://jasonvincent10.github.io/last-person-standing/. Log in, then tap **Host**.

## Before a game
1. **New players** sign up themselves from the link. They show as **Awaiting payment**.
2. **When someone pays you:**
   - Host → **Players**, then tap their name.
   - Match the bank reference (e.g. `LPS-12-SAM`) to the player.
   - Tap **+1** for £5, **+2** for £10, and so on.
3. **Start the game:** Host → **Game** → **Start new game**.
   - Everyone with credit is entered automatically, and 1 credit is taken from each.
   - Anyone left on 0 stays **Awaiting payment**.
   - Paid late? Players → their name → **Enter into current game**.

## Every week
**Monday to Friday (before 5pm Friday)**
- **This week** shows who has and hasn't picked. Chase emails go out automatically (Wednesday, Thursday and Friday at 9am by default).
- **FA Cup or odd weekend?** Set the week type:
  - **Normal:** every club the player hasn't used yet.
  - **Week off:** no picks and no emails.
  - **Custom:** only the clubs you list. You can add non-league sides.
  - If you change the type after people have picked, anyone whose club is no longer allowed has to pick again, and they get that club back.
- **Someone emails a pick after 5pm?** Use **Override a pick**. It works after the deadline.

**Friday 5pm.** Picks lock, and everyone can see everyone's picks on **Overview**.

**After the last match (usually Monday night)**
1. Go to **This week** → **Results**. Tap **Won** or **Lost** for each club. A draw counts as **Lost**.
2. **Postponed or abandoned match?** Tap **Postponed** on that club and set a new deadline. Those players pick again, and they get their old club back. Results can't be confirmed until they've re-picked or the new deadline has passed.
3. Tap **Confirm results**. Anyone on a red club, or with no pick, is **out**.
   *Made a mistake? Tap **Undo results**, but only before opening the next week.*
4. **Copy summary** gives you a line like *"64 in, 22 out this week: Blackburn (9), QPR (8)…"* to paste into the group.
5. Tap **Open next gameweek**. The deadline defaults to next Friday at 5pm.

## Ending a game
When one player is left (or everyone goes out in the same week): Host → **Game** → **End game**.
- Tick the winner(s) and add a note, e.g. "Split pot".
- The game is saved in **History**, and you can start the next one straight away.

## Every summer
Host → **Clubs**: move promoted and relegated clubs between divisions. Aim for 20 / 24 / 24 / 24, and check the counts at the top.

## Settings
- **Payment details:** the bank details players see. Only logged-in players can see them.
- **Chase emails:**
  - switch them on or off
  - choose the days and times (UK time)
  - edit the wording; `{name}`, `{deadline}`, `{gameweek}` and `{link}` fill in automatically
  - **Send me a test email**, or **Send chase now**
- **Another host:** Players → their name → **Make host**.

## Good to know
- Money never goes through the app. It only tracks **credit** (1 credit = 1 game).
- Players can't see anyone else's pick until 5pm Friday. Hosts **can** see this week's picks on the Host screen (needed for overrides), so keep them to yourself until the deadline.
- Missed the deadline with no pick = out, unless you override it.
- Use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else. A host account can change the bank details.
